About This Site

A live sports command center — real odds, real line movement, real player props, all in one place. Built for tracking games and prices across every major league without switching between a dozen different apps.

Our data policy
Every number on this site is either pulled live from a real public data source, or calculated directly from that real data. Nothing here is simulated, estimated, or made up. If a real price or stat isn't available yet for something, the site says so plainly instead of guessing or showing a placeholder.
Where the data comes from
Kalshi Real moneylines, spreads, totals, player props, and price history for MLB, NFL, NBA, NHL, NCAAF, and WNBA.
ESPN Live scores, schedules, team records, and in-game stats.
Open-Meteo Real current weather and wind conditions for outdoor games.
